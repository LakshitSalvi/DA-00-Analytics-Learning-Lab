# 🏢 About Airbnb SQL Interviews

Airbnb uses SQL across several teams including:

- Product Analytics
- Marketplace Intelligence
- Guest Experience
- Host Experience
- Pricing
- Search & Discovery
- Growth
- Trust & Safety

SQL interviews focus on understanding how users search, book, and interact with the marketplace.

Interviewers evaluate:

- SQL Fundamentals
- Product Thinking
- Marketplace Analytics
- Business Metrics
- Communication
- Query Optimization

# 💡 Pro Tips

✔ Think about both guests and hosts.

✔ Clearly define conversion metrics.

✔ Explain assumptions before coding.

✔ Consider seasonal effects.

✔ Handle duplicate booking events.

✔ Discuss scalability.

✔ Keep SQL modular using CTEs.

✔ Relate every query to a business decision.

---

# 🏆 Final Advice

Airbnb SQL interviews emphasize solving marketplace and product analytics problems.

Strong candidates understand how to analyze booking behavior, pricing, host performance, and customer retention using clean, scalable SQL. Always connect your analysis to business outcomes such as increasing bookings, improving host quality, and enhancing the guest experience.
