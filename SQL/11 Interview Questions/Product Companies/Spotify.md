Spotify uses SQL extensively across teams including:

- Product Analytics

- Music Intelligence
- Growth

SQL interviews focus on understanding how users discover, consume, and engage with music and podcasts.

Interviewers evaluate:

- SQL Fundamentals

- Communication

# 💡 Pro Tips

✔ Define the product metric before writing SQL.

✔ Explain user behavior, not just numbers.

✔ Think about recommendations and personalization.

✔ Handle duplicate streaming events carefully.

✔ Consider long-term retention.

✔ Discuss scalability.

✔ Use CTEs for readability.

✔ Connect every metric to improving the listening experience.

---

# 🏆 Final Advice

Spotify SQL interviews emphasize understanding how users interact with music, podcasts, and recommendations.

Strong candidates combine SQL expertise with product thinking, using data to explain user engagement, content discovery, retention, and experimentation. Focus on writing clean, scalable SQL while clearly communicating the business impact of your analysis.
