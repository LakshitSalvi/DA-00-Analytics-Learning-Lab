# 🏢 About Uber SQL Interviews

Uber uses SQL across multiple organizations including:

- Product Analytics
- Marketplace Intelligence
- Rider Experience
- Driver Operations
- Eats Analytics
- Pricing
- Growth
- Business Intelligence

SQL interviews focus on solving **real marketplace problems** where two sides of the platform—drivers and riders—must be analyzed together.

Interviewers evaluate:

- SQL Fundamentals
- Analytical Thinking
- Product Sense
- Business Metrics
- Query Optimization
- Communication

# 💡 Pro Tips

✔ Think about both riders and drivers.

✔ Define the business metric before writing SQL.

✔ Explain assumptions clearly.

✔ Handle duplicate events carefully.

✔ Consider city-level segmentation.

✔ Discuss scalability.

✔ Keep SQL modular with CTEs.

✔ Always connect results to product decisions.

---

# 🏆 Final Advice

Uber SQL interviews are designed to evaluate how well you understand marketplace dynamics.

Strong candidates can transform ride, driver, and user activity into actionable insights using clean, efficient SQL. Focus not only on writing the correct query, but also on explaining how your analysis would improve marketplace efficiency, customer experience, and business growth.
