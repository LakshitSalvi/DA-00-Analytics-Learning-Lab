# EY SQL Interview Guide

> A comprehensive roadmap for preparing for **EY SQL interviews**, covering financial reporting, auditing, compliance analytics, governance, and the SQL concepts commonly evaluated in EY's consulting and analytics roles.

---

# 🏢 About EY SQL Interviews

EY uses SQL across multiple business areas including:

- Technology Consulting
- Data & Analytics
- Financial Advisory
- Risk Consulting
- Assurance
- Tax Technology
- Business Consulting
- AI & Data Services

Unlike product companies, EY interviews focus on using SQL to solve **business, finance, audit, and compliance problems**.

Interviewers evaluate:

- SQL Fundamentals
- Business Understanding
- Financial Reporting
- Data Accuracy
- Risk Analysis
- Communication Skills

# 💡 Pro Tips

✔ Verify calculations carefully.

✔ Explain business impact.

✔ Think about auditability.

✔ Handle missing and duplicate data.

✔ Keep SQL modular using CTEs.

✔ Validate assumptions.

✔ Focus on accuracy before optimization.

✔ Present results in a business-friendly way.

---

# 🏆 Final Advice

EY SQL interviews focus on producing reliable analyses that support financial reporting, risk management, and business decision-making.

Strong candidates combine solid SQL fundamentals with an understanding of governance, compliance, and business processes. Always explain not only how your query works, but also how it helps stakeholders make informed decisions.
