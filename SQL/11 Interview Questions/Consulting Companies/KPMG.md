# KPMG SQL Interview Guide

> A comprehensive roadmap for preparing for **KPMG SQL interviews**, covering audit analytics, financial reporting, governance, compliance, risk management, and the SQL concepts most frequently evaluated in consulting and advisory roles.

---

# 🏢 About KPMG SQL Interviews

KPMG uses SQL across several business areas including:

- Data & Analytics
- Audit Technology
- Risk Consulting
- Financial Advisory
- Technology Consulting
- Digital Transformation
- Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)
- Business Intelligence

SQL interviews focus on using data to solve business and regulatory problems while ensuring accuracy and reliability.

Interviewers evaluate:

- SQL Fundamentals
- Business Understanding
- Analytical Thinking
- Reporting Skills
- Data Validation
- Communication

# 💡 Pro Tips

✔ Verify every calculation.

✔ Think about auditability.

✔ Explain business impact.

✔ Validate assumptions.

✔ Handle NULL values correctly.

✔ Keep SQL modular using CTEs.

✔ Consider performance on large datasets.

✔ Present findings clearly for business stakeholders.

---

# 🏆 Final Advice

KPMG SQL interviews emphasize accuracy, governance, and business problem-solving.

Successful candidates combine strong SQL fundamentals with the ability to validate data, identify risks, and produce reliable reports that support audits, compliance activities, and executive decision-making.

Focus on writing SQL that is not only correct, but also trustworthy, maintainable, and easy to explain.
