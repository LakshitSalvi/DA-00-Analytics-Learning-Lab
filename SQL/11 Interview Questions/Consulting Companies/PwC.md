# 🏢 About PwC SQL Interviews

PwC uses SQL across business areas such as:

- Data & Analytics
- Technology Consulting
- Risk Assurance
- Financial Advisory
- Tax Technology
- Business Transformation
- Digital Consulting
- Audit Analytics

SQL interviews focus on using data to solve real client problems.

Interviewers evaluate:

- SQL Fundamentals
- Business Thinking
- Reporting Skills
- Data Accuracy
- Analytical Reasoning
- Communication

# 💡 Pro Tips

✔ Think from a client's perspective.

✔ Validate your calculations.

✔ Keep SQL readable.

✔ Explain business insights.

✔ Use CTEs to simplify complex reports.

✔ Handle NULL values carefully.

✔ Verify aggregated results.

✔ Focus on decision-making, not just query writing.

---

# 🏆 Final Advice

PwC SQL interviews are designed to assess whether you can turn raw business data into meaningful insights.

Strong candidates write SQL that is accurate, maintainable, and easy to explain while demonstrating how the results support executive decision-making, financial reporting, and client recommendations.
